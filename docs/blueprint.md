# **App Name**: VenueFlow

## Core Features:

- Interactive Venue Map: A dynamic map displaying real-time crowd density, points of interest (restrooms, concessions), and optimal walking paths to help attendees navigate efficiently.
- Real-time Queue Predictor: Utilizes a predictive AI tool to estimate wait times for popular amenities such as restrooms, concession stands, and entry gates based on live data.
- Personalized Event Itinerary: An AI-powered tool that suggests optimal times and routes for attendees to visit various points of interest, events, or performances based on their preferences and venue conditions.
- Contextual Alerts & Updates: Delivers real-time push notifications for important venue information, including gate changes, special promotions, security alerts, and crowd advisories.
- Mobile Concession Ordering: Enables attendees to browse menus, order food and beverages from their seats, and pick up their items at designated, expedited collection points to reduce waiting times.
- Emergency & Safety Guidance: Provides instant access to venue-specific emergency procedures, clear evacuation routes, and locations of first-aid stations to ensure attendee safety.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A confident and clear blue (#176BD8) to represent guidance, technology, and trustworthiness, reminiscent of dynamic sporting environments.
- Background color: A very light, desaturated grey-blue (#E6EDF3) that provides a clean, open canvas for content, ensuring readability in various lighting conditions.
- Accent color: A vibrant cyan (#4DD9D9) used for highlights, calls to action, and real-time updates to signify dynamism and attract attention to critical information.
- All text: 'Inter', a versatile sans-serif font, for its modern, highly legible characteristics suitable for displaying data-rich content and ensuring clarity on small screens.
- Utilize a suite of clean, geometric, and intuitively recognizable vector icons to facilitate quick comprehension of navigation points and service offerings.
- A mobile-first, card-based layout designed for quick scanning and easy interaction, ensuring a logical information hierarchy and accessibility to critical features on the go.
- Subtle and purposeful animations for map transitions, content loading, and real-time data updates, guiding the user's focus without distraction.
